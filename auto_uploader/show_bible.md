# Stackswopo show bible

Every AI step that picks, cuts or judges a clip reads this file first.
Edit it in plain words whenever something changes: a new character, a
new running joke, a clip that blew up. Keep it short; it is read on
every run.

## Who he is
- Stackswopo ("Stacks", "Wop"). Black streamer from Brooklyn, loud,
  quick, never breaks the bit. Streams on YouTube, clips go to
  Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Shorts and Rumble.
- Main content: GTA roleplay on a city RP server. Also "monkey"
  streams (random video chat with strangers), reacts (boxing, BrandRisk,
  GTA 6 trailers), NBA 2K, and just talking to chat.

## Characters and situations he plays in GTA RP
- He is the main character of every stream - the star, whatever
  character he is playing that night. He switches characters; JOHNNY
  COX is one of them (there was a "Johnny Cox Funeral" stream on
  4/25/26). Other players call him by the character's name, so a line
  addressed to that name is addressed to him, and his character is the
  one the clip is about. In titles and captions he is "Stacks"; use the
  character's name only when the clip is about the character.
- The PASTOR: runs a church and treats it like a hustle. Charges a
  thousand-dollar tithe because you look rich, demands 40%, makes people
  the topic of the sermon, has a dress code, gets exposed by cops in
  front of the congregation. Holy words, criminal logic.
- The CRIMINAL / CLIENT: always getting pulled over, cuffed or arrested,
  and always negotiating. "What do y'all do with my client", "we gonna
  take this down to the court", "my arresting officer is twelve years
  old", asks for his lawyer, claims the body cam is off.
- Casino and blackjack scenes, hospital/EMS scenes, barbershop scenes,
  robberies and gang arguments.

## What makes him funny
- Absurd logic said with total confidence: selling drugs outside the
  church "to save lives", a pastor taking a cut.
- Fast comebacks and roasts ("you dress like a judge", "them eyes going
  through a divorce", "that's not a mohawk, that's the hot taper").
- Movie and pop-culture riffs (the Goodfellas "funny how?" bit).
- Playing dumb with cops; turning an arrest into a negotiation.
- Big honest reactions when a stranger or another player says something
  wild.

## What his audience shares (real top posts)
- Short, quotable one-liners: "Imma switch yo ahh" (4M views),
  "stop wait a minute" (1.6M), "hi @50 Cent" (761K), "do ts on a block",
  "Jamaica mannn", "Santa gon make it happen no matter what",
  "i can't wait to play house", "They damned me to hell".
- Most hits are 9 to 40 seconds: one setup, one line, one reaction.

## What does NOT work
- Him reading chat, setting up, loading screens, driving around.
- Rants or arguments that are mostly slurs with no joke in them.
- Scenes that need the last hour of the stream to make sense.
- Five clips from the same room and the same conversation.
- A joke cut off before the reaction, or starting in the middle of a
  different bit.
